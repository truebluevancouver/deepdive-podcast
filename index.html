ABOUT

Big questions. Real conversations.

The Deep Dive with Daniel Thiele is a conservative political and current-affairs podcast hosted by Daniel Thiele and Stephen Lowy. The show explores Canadian politics, free speech, personal responsibility, public safety, culture, government and the ideas shaping the country — going beyond short headlines and sound bites.

HOSTS

Daniel Thiele & Stephen Lowy

CO-HOST

Stephen Lowy

Stephen brings his perspective to conversations about politics, public affairs, Canadian identity and the ideas behind the news.

CO-HOST

Daniel Thiele

Daniel co-hosts The Deep Dive with a focus on conservative politics, interviews, free speech and getting beneath the surface of major stories.

GUESTS

Long-form conversations.

Guest interviews will feature political figures, commentators, advocates and people with first-hand experience of the issues being discussed.
