ABOUT THE SHOW

Big questions. Real conversations.

The Deep Dive with Daniel Thiele is a conservative political and current-affairs podcast hosted by Daniel Thiele and Stephen Lowy. The show focuses on Canada, free speech, personal responsibility, limited government, public safety, culture, and the political ideas shaping the country — while going beyond short headlines and sound bites.
